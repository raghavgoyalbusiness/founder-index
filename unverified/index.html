How to clear one

  1. Open the link. If there isn't one, find out whether the thing exists at all.
  2. Check the description is true — and that it says what the tool does for you, in twelve words or fewer.
  3. In data/resources.json, drop "verify": true, add"lastChecked" with today's date, and put your handle in"addedBy".
  4. Open a pull request. That's the whole job.
