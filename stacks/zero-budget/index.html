Zero-budget stack

You have a laptop, no money, and something you want to put in front of people.

The reasoning

Every tool here has a free tier you can build a real product on, not a trial that expires in fourteen days. The trap at this stage is spending three weeks choosing infrastructure for a product nobody has asked for yet — so this stack is deliberately boring in the places that don't matter. Take the free model credits, put the database somewhere that scales to zero, and spend the saved money on nothing at all. You will know within a month whether it deserves a budget.
