Launch week

The thing works and nobody knows it exists.

The reasoning

Launching is not one day, it is about nine posts over two weeks, and the order matters. Warm up somewhere forgiving first — Indie Hackers or a Show HN — so the feedback lands before the big one. Product Hunt rewards preparation and punishes improvisation, so pick the date early and line the rest up behind it. The alternative-to listings are the quiet winner here: they do nothing on the day and then send you traffic for two years.
