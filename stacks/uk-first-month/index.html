UK founder's first month

You have registered a company in the UK, or you are about to.

The reasoning

The UK is unusually generous to early companies if you do the paperwork in the right order, and unusually punishing if you don't. Get SEIS advance assurance before you take anyone's money, not after — investors will ask, and doing it retroactively is where founders lose rounds. Read the equity guides before you promise a co-founder a number. Innovate UK is the one that surprises people: it is real money that costs you no equity, and the applications are long but not hard.
