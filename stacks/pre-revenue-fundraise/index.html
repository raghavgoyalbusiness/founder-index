Pre-revenue fundraise

You are raising before you have meaningful revenue to point at.

The reasoning

Without revenue, the round is sold on the team, the wedge, and evidence you can move quickly — which means your materials carry more weight than they will ever carry again. Benchmark the terms before the first call so you can tell a normal offer from a bad one. And exhaust the non-dilutive money first: a grant or a fellowship is the only funding that improves your position rather than diluting it, and both look better on the cap table than a rushed angel round.
